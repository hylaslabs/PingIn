Privacy Policy for PingIn

Last updated: 19 September 2026

PingIn ("the app") is an attendance tracking app developed by [YOUR NAME].

Information we collect

PingIn does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. All data you enter (subjects, schedules, attendance records, and settings) is stored locally on your device only. We do not have access to it.

Permissions

  • Notifications: used to show local attendance reminders that you schedule. These are generated on your device and are not sent through any server.
  • Vibration: used for haptic feedback when you interact with the app.

Third parties

PingIn does not use analytics, advertising, or any third-party services that collect data.

Data deletion

Because all data is stored on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Children's privacy

PingIn does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
